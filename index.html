ApycueAug 2025 – now

Designing an AI-agent platform for hotels, as a team of one

Independent hotels lose a large share of each booking to OTA commissions. Apycue helps them win bookings directly, with their own website, marketing and AI agents that follow up on guest enquiries.

RoleSole product designer
ScopeWhole product, 0 to 1
TeamFounders and developers
ToolsFigma, Figma MCP, Claude
10+hotel clients onboarded in the first year
40–50%less design time for new features
6+website templates powering the builder

The problem

Hotel teams are busy and not technical. An AI agent that talks to their guests is only useful if the team can see what it did, trust it, and step in when needed. The product had to make agent behaviour clear without making the hotel team learn how AI works.

My role

I own the product end to end: product and feature discussions, information architecture, user flows, the visual language (colour and typography), every screen, and design QA with developers.

Key decisions

  1. Show the agent's work, not just its result. Each conversation shows what the agent did and why, so the hotel team can trust it and take over when a human is needed.
  2. Build the foundation first. I set up the visual language and a design system from scratch, so new features could ship fast and stay consistent.
  3. Use AI to go faster, not to decide. I use Claude and the Figma MCP to explore best practices and generate starting screens. The final design decisions stay with me.
  4. Stay with the build. I write UI specs, review built screens against the design, and flag UX and flow issues before release.

6+ website templates that let any hotel launch a direct-booking site

A hotel's own website is where direct bookings start. I designed the templates behind Apycue's website builder, so hotels get a polished, SEO-friendly site without starting from scratch.

RoleSole designer
Output6+ templates
Used byApycue's website builder
FocusDirect bookings, SEO
6+templates designed for the builder
10+hotels onboarded onto Apycue in year one
SEOfriendly structure built into every template

The problem

Many independent hotels have outdated websites that send guests to OTAs. They need a site that looks like their brand and pushes guests to book direct, but they don't have a design team.

Key decisions

  1. Different looks, one system. Every template is built on the same components, so a hotel can change its look without rebuilding its content.
  2. Booking first. Each template keeps the path to a direct booking visible on every page.
  3. SEO from the start. The page structure is set up for search in the product, so hotels get it without extra work.

Aureate Labs · Ayu Developers2020 – 2025

60+ online stores, from home page to checkout

Five years of designing B2C e-commerce: the full shopping journey, the design systems that kept it consistent, and later leading junior designers on it.

RoleSenior UI/UX Designer
ScopeFull shopping journey
TeamDevelopers, PMs, junior designers
ToolsFigma, Adobe XD
30+stores designed end to end at Aureate Labs
35+web and mobile projects led at Ayu Developers
1design system per project, set before the inner pages

What I did

At Aureate Labs I designed stores end to end: home page, product pages, cart and checkout. I ran usability testing and changed designs based on what we learned.

At Ayu Developers I moved into a senior role. I designed each project's home page and set its design system, colours and typography. Junior designers then built the inner pages on that system, and I reviewed their work and helped them improve it.

Key decisions

  1. Design system first. Setting colours, type and components before the inner pages kept 35+ projects consistent and made junior designers faster.
  2. Checkout gets the most care. It is the step where a store wins or loses the sale, so it got the most testing and iteration.
  3. Review, don't redo. Giving junior designers specific feedback raised quality across the team instead of creating a bottleneck.
